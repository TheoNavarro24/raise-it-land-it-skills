# Frame: fictional cycle 1 input

Start a new practice conversation with the installed Frame skill active. Send the start input, then each fixed learner reply only when its matching question is asked. These are fictional demonstration inputs, not prescribed learner answers. The facilitator controls the preparation window.

## Start input

```text
Help me prepare for cycle 1 formative practice with Frame. This is a public fictional demonstration brief, not an assessed case. The facilitator's preparation window is open.

Public learner brief: A fictional organisation piloted a process change in two of its six teams. It is considering extending the change to the remaining teams. You are preparing to discuss the proposed extension with the project lead. You have no private counterpart information. Form your own position; the brief does not prescribe a resolution.

Please ask the four preparation questions one at a time and record my words.
```

## Reply when asked: What is your evidence?

```text
Only two of the six teams were in the pilot.
```

## Reply when asked: What do you think they may be protecting?

```text
I don't know.
```

## Reply when asked: What is your opening line?

```text
I worry about the teams that were not in the pilot.
```

## Reply when asked: What would change your mind?

```text
A small test with those teams that shows no increase in errors.
```

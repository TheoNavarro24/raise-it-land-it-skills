# Frame course scope

Version: 0.1.1

MDOE407's **Raise It, Land It** is a proposed course in sustaining a constructive disagreement and reaching an owned decision. Frame supports articulation before cycle 1 formative practice. A person plays the counterpart; a person conducts the debrief and determines assessment. Frame has no coaching, simulation, transcript-coding or grading role.

The learner works from a **public fictional learner brief at runtime** and may attach it or confirm that they have it. Frame records the learner's answers rather than copying case facts into the card. No case-specific facts, model openings, private counterpart information or worked solutions are bundled here. The learner supplies evidence, a hypothesis about what the counterpart may be protecting, an opening line, and what could change their view. Keeping a concern with reasons is permitted; Frame does not prescribe agreement or concession.

The facilitator controls a common eight-minute preparation window, including any agreed access adjustment. The learner can retain their own card during their player run. At the end of preparation, the facilitator collects or locks cards and returns each at the relevant run; this plugin cannot perform or verify that control. Learners put the card aside and do not revise it while observing earlier rounds.

Cycle 2 preparation and the assessed call are independent, without AI or other automated assistance. Frame should decline requests to use its workflow for them. Instructions alone cannot prevent a person using another tool or account; course delivery and access arrangements remain the institution's responsibility.

Use an institution-approved environment and fictional roles. A fresh conversation containing only the public learner brief is recommended. This is a practical separation measure, not a claim that memory is disabled, past exposure is erased, or access is secure. Avoid identifiable workplace, colleague, client or customer data. Ordinary fictional disagreements about work are the scope; real misconduct and safety incidents need their appropriate procedures.

If the assistant is unavailable or the preparation window ends, use a manual card with the same four fields. Missing answers stay **Not stated**. No preparation card establishes that the learner demonstrated the course outcomes during a call.

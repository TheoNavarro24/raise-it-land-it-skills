# Expected behaviour: review checks, not test results

These intended checks do not assert that a provider has passed them.

## Frame example

Ask the four questions in order, one at a time. Record the four replies literally; “I don't know.” is an answer. Return the four-field card automatically after reply four. Do not suggest motives, improve the opening, grade, coach or infer answers from the brief. Skip/early-card missing fields stay Not stated. The facilitator controls timing; ended preparation stops assisted questions/edits. Only explicitly named field replacements are allowed while open. Cycle 2/assessed preparation is unsupported.

## Trace examples

Every review includes M1, M2a, M2b, M3, M4 and M5; exact Player quotes/locators; Counterpart context separately; the five-part M5 checklist; limits/tutor checks; and one short reflection question. Reasoned refinement or retention may evidence M2b; concession is unnecessary. The transcript is constant across the three cases; only public feasibility context changes.

| Input | Expected feasibility disposition |
|---|---|
| V01_feasible | Authority and editing capacity before Thursday are explicit public context. Full M5 support is available if all other required components are evidenced. |
| V02_unknown | Retain visible decision, owner, review condition and agreement. Feasibility uncertain; M5 Partial evidence. Ownership/agreement alone do not establish capability. |
| V03_infeasible | Retain visible components but mark feasibility contradicted; identify the Thursday/Monday conflict and withhold full support. |

Tutor verifies quotations, conditions and interpretation; no numerical grade, pass/fail, personality judgement, ideal answer or replacement script. Missing record/context is distinct from absent behaviour; withholding/coaching requires supported tutor opportunity flags, not an automatic fairness verdict. No hidden case facts or other runs may fill gaps.

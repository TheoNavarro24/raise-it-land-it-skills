# What to check after each run

Prototype 0.1.3. All 33 cases are **not run**. These are expectations, not measured results. Read this page after testing; do not paste it into the agent’s chat or attach it as skill knowledge.

## Checks for every Frame case

- Recording-only cycle 1 public fictional scope; exact literal learner words, no praise/polish/coaching/inferred motives or grade. At most one neutral content clarification per attempt.
- After one brief prototype orientation, questions are exact and asked one at a time, next unanswered only; automatic four-field card on completion/request/end-window; missing/skipped fields Not stated.

## Checks for every Trace case

- Load required resources or stop naming unavailable resource. Include M1,M2a,M2b,M3,M4,M5, exact Player quotes/locators, coverage/context limits, separately labelled Counterpart context, all-five M5 checklist, tutor flags and one reflection question.
- M5 full support requires decision, owner, observable review condition, agreement and explicit feasibility without contradiction; apply to follow-ups too. Coaching/opportunity distinct from component completeness.
- Advisory tutor-check output, no grade/pass/fail/personality/motive/ideal answer/script. No privacy/consent/fairness guarantees.
